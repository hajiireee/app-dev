| Arcs |Setting |
| ----------- | ----------- |
| East blue saga | Monkey D. Luffy gathers his first crewmates, including Zoro, Nami, Usopp, and Sanji, and enters the Grand Line. |
| Alabasta Saga |The crew navigates the Grand Line, gains their doctor Chopper, and liberates the desert kingdom of Alabasta from the Warlord Crocodile. Nico Robin joins the crew. |
|Sky Island Saga  |The Straw Hats venture high into the sky to a legendary island, fighting the formidable enemy God Enel. |
| Water 7 Saga | An emotional arc where the crew faces internal conflict, travels to the shipbuilding city of Water 7, and wages war on the World Government at Enies Lobby to save Robin. Franky joins the crew.|
| Thriller Bark Saga | The crew enters the mysterious sea known as the Florian Triangle and crosses paths with the Warlord Gecko Moria, gaining Brook as their musician.|
|Summit War Saga  | The crew is disastrously separated, Luffy fights through the great prison Impel Down, and an all-out war between the Whitebeard Pirates and the Marines erupts, resulting in a world-changing loss.|
| Fish-Man Island Saga |The Straw Hats reconvene after a two-year time-skip and journey beneath the Red Line to the Fish-Man kingdom to enter the New World. |
|Dressrosa Saga  | Luffy forms an alliance with Trafalgar Law and battles the Warlord Donquixote Doflamingo to free the kingdom of Dressrosa.|
|Whole Cake Island Saga  |The crew journeys to the mysterious elephant island of Zou and then infiltrates the territory of the Emperor Big Mom to rescue Sanji. |
|Wano Country Saga  |The Straw Hats, along with their allies, wage an epic war to liberate the isolated country of Wano from the grip of the Emperor Kaido. |
| EggHead |The current, ongoing saga that begins to wrap up the main storylines as the quest for the One Piece and the truth of the world nears its end. |
